Why it works

Good intentions need good timing.

Most managers know what a good onboarding looks like. The problem is remembering it on the right Tuesday. Onboarding Nudges takes the timing off your plate, and each nudge is built on what the research says matters most in a new start.

  1. 01

    Belonging comes first

    Feeling expected and connected early is one of the strongest signals of whether someone settles in. So the programme starts before Day 1, with the team.

  2. 02

    Clarity beats volume

    New hires don’t need more information. They need to know what matters and what good looks like. The early nudges keep priorities clear.

  3. 03

    Doubt is normal, silence is costly

    Around week two, people become aware of everything they don’t know yet, and many stop asking. The nudges name it early and make it safe to ask.

  4. 04

    Promises get checked

    If the job doesn’t match what was described in the interview, it shows up in the first month or two. The nudges help you ask about it before it becomes a reason to leave.

  5. 05

    Ownership keeps people

    Autonomy and a visible future matter more over time than another checklist. The final nudges hand over ownership and talk about what’s next.

Each nudge’s action guide includes a research anchor naming its sources, including Gallup, Harvard Business Review, the Journal of Applied Psychology, LinkedIn’s Workplace Learning Report and Self-Determination Theory (Deci & Ryan).
