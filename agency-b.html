The gap

The relationship doesn’t end when they start.

When a placement fails early, you wear the cost: in fees, reputation, and relationships.

You put weeks into finding the right person and convincing them the role was right. From day one, whether they stay depends on how well they’re brought in, and that sits with someone else.

Onboarding Nudges gives that someone a little help, at the moments that matter.

Figures we cite

  • 88% of organisations do onboarding poorly, by their own admission. Gallup, 2023
  • $30k+ average cost to replace a single mid-level employee. Gallup, 2024
