The Team Signal

“Your new hire starts next week. Is the team ready?”

Why this matters

A team that wasn’t told someone is starting next week will treat that person like an interruption. A team that was told will treat them like they were expected.

The team alert

“Heads up, [name] starts next week. Please stop by on their first morning to introduce yourself. Make them feel like we were expecting them.”
