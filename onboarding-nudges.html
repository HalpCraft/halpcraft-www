01The problem

Most managers know what a good start looks like.

  • MondayRosters, and a supplier who won’t call back.
  • TuesdayThe inbox.
  • WednesdayThree things due by Friday.
  • ThursdayCheck in with the new starter.

The problem is remembering it on the right Tuesday. The conversations that make a new start work are small, and they’re the easiest thing in the week to let slip.

Questions

Not covered here? hello@halpcraft.com. A real person replies within 24 hours.

Is this a subscription?

No. You pay once for each new hire and nothing renews. $49 NZD for one, or $220 NZD for five and $390 NZD for ten.

Which calendars does it work with?

Google Calendar, Outlook and Apple Calendar. You get one calendar file by email and import it once.

Does my new hire see any of this?

No. The nudges are for you, the manager. HalpCraft never contacts your new hire.

My new hire works shifts. Does it still work?

Yes. Choose the shift pattern during setup and your email includes a link to adjust any nudge date to fit the roster before you import the calendar. Standard schedules skip weekends automatically.

How much time does each nudge take?

Most take 10 to 20 minutes. Day 1 is the longest at about an hour, and most of that is time spent with your new hire, which you’d be spending anyway.

I’m buying for several managers. How does that work?

Buy a Team 5 or Team 10 pack and you’ll receive setup codes by email. Pass one to each manager. They enter it at halpcraft.com/redeem, fill in their own new hire’s details, and their nudges go to their calendar. Codes are valid for 12 months.
